How it works

Hourly tilted solar irradiance forecast × 3.99 kWp × PV performance factor gives estimated generation. Subtract estimated daytime house use, apply a forecast safety margin and charging efficiency, then reserve enough battery space for the expected solar surplus.

Conservative rule: if the forecast is unavailable, no charging recommendation is issued. Real daytime consumption timing, battery charge limits and shading are not yet modelled. If your inverter does not support a partial target, do not use this percentage as a charge-duration instruction.

Weather data: Open-Meteo. Forecasts are estimates, not guarantees. For personal use.
