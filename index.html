How Version 2 works

Uses tomorrow's hourly tilted solar irradiance to estimate PV output. Simulates solar supply, household demand and battery charging/discharging in half-hour steps from 05:30 to 23:30, testing overnight targets from 10% to 99%. Chooses the least estimated grid electricity cost, applying a solar safety margin.

Important: The consumption profile is an estimate, not yet measured hourly demand. This version does not read your Octopus or Soltaro accounts, and does not control the inverter. Check forecasts and battery settings yourself. If the forecast fails, no new target is issued.

Assumes cheap-rate household usage is supplied from the grid while force-charging, no battery discharge during that window, and no payment for additional exported solar because your FIT export is deemed. Weather: Open-Meteo.
