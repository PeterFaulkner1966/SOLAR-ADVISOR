How V3 works

Models each forecast hour from 05:30 to 23:30, with proportional half-hours at the start/end. Solar serves household load first, then charges the battery. Battery supplies remaining household demand down to 10%. The model compares targets from 10% to 99%, balancing overnight purchase cost, peak-rate imports and unused solar.

This is an advisory estimate, not an inverter control or a guarantee. The hourly household demand pattern is estimated. Battery power limits, unusual appliance use and solar forecast errors can change the best target. If forecasts fail, no target is issued.
